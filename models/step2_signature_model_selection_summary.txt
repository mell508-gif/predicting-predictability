Step 2 Signature Autoencoder Model Selection
===========================================

Models compared:
- 64D no dropout, trained for 50 epochs
- 128D no dropout, trained for 50 epochs

Conclusion:
The 128D signature autoencoder achieved the stronger validation performance. Although validation loss increased after its best epoch, the best-validation checkpoint at epoch 18 had lower validation loss and higher nonzero sign accuracy than the 64D model. Therefore, the 128D best-validation checkpoint will be used for downstream signature embeddings.
